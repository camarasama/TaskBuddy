How it works

Assign, prove, reward.

You assign the task

Create chores with a difficulty rating and assign them to one child or several. Set them to repeat.

They prove it's done

Your child marks it complete and uploads a photo, so "I did it" stops being something to argue about.

You approve, they earn

Approve or send it back with a note. Approved work pays out XP for levelling and points to spend.

What makes it stick

Built around how families actually work.

⭐

Two currencies, on purpose

XP drives levels and never gets spent, so progress never goes backwards. Points are the spendable currency. A child can save up for something big without losing the level they climbed to.

📷

Photo evidence before points

Completion needs proof, and you approve it. That single step removes most of the friction about whether a job was really finished — or finished properly.

🔑

No email address for kids

Children sign in with a memorable family code and a PIN — nothing like MEGA-VIPER-8481 requires giving a ten-year-old an inbox.

👪

Both parents, equal access

Invite a partner, co-parent or guardian and they get full management access. Chores aren't one parent's admin job.

🎁

Rewards with real limits

Set what points can buy, cap redemptions per child and per household, and add expiry dates — so the reward catalogue stays something you can actually honour.

📊

See who's drifting

Streaks, achievements and completion reports show which child is falling behind while it's still a small conversation.
